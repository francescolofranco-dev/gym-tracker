Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Gym Tracker (package dev.lofrancolabs.gymtracker) is developed by Francesco Lo Franco ("Lofranco Labs"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

Gym Tracker does not collect, sell or share personal data. The developer has no servers and receives none of your data. There are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your workouts, exercises, templates, sets, notes and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled for this app. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks Google for permission to use the drive.appdata scope. Backups are stored in a hidden app-specific folder in your own Google Drive, which only Gym Tracker can access. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive. The developer never receives these backups. You can disconnect at any time in the app's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the stored backup from Google Drive settings under "Manage apps".

Gym Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

File backups and sharing

File backups are saved only where you choose. Sharing a session summary sends text only to the app you pick, such as a messaging app.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only for the optional Google Drive backup.
  • Notifications and foreground service: show and control the workout timer.
  • Display over other apps: only if you enable the floating timer.

Children

Gym Tracker is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
