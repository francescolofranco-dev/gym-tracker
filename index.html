Gym Tracker

A focused Android workout log for sets, reps, weight, notes and progress over time.

  • Blank or template-based sessions, with unilateral left/right sets.
  • Exercise notes that carry into your next workout, plus pinned notes.
  • Progress charts, personal records and weekly muscle coverage.
  • Workout timer with notification, Quick Settings tile and optional floating counter.
  • Backups to a file or to your own Google Drive.

Your workouts stay on your phone. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.
