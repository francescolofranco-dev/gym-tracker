Gym Tracker

Track your lifts. See your progress.

Log sets, reps, and weights and see how your lifts change over time. No login or personal details required. Workout logging and progress tracking work offline.

  • Blank or template-based sessions, with unilateral left/right sets.
  • Exercise notes that carry into your next workout, plus pinned notes.
  • Progress charts, personal records and weekly muscle coverage.
  • Workout timer with notification, Quick Settings tile and optional floating counter.

Your workout history is saved on your device. You can back it up to a file or connect Google Drive for optional cloud backup. Gym Tracker has no ads or analytics.
